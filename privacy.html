THEERAM • PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Theeram may process when you use the Theeram Discord bot.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Overview

Theeram is a Discord music bot. We aim to collect and process only the information necessary to provide the bot's functionality.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed, why it may be processed, how it may be shared, how it may be retained, and how you can request deletion of information associated with you.

2. Information Theeram May Process

Discord Information

When you interact with Theeram through Discord, the bot may receive information made available to it through Discord's API, such as:

  • Discord user ID
  • Discord server/guild ID
  • Discord channel or voice-channel information
  • User interactions with Theeram commands

Music Requests

When you use a music command, Theeram may process the search query or URL you provide so that it can locate and play the requested content.

Temporary Playback Information

While music is playing, Theeram may temporarily process information such as the current track, queue, playback state, volume, and voice connection state.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Theeram does not intentionally request or collect:

  • Your Discord password.
  • Your Discord authentication token.
  • Your payment card information.
  • Private messages unrelated to Theeram's functionality.
  • Personal information that is not necessary for the bot's operation.

Never provide your Discord password, authentication token, or other account credentials to Theeram.

4. How Information Is Used

Information available to Theeram is used only as necessary to provide and maintain its functionality.

This may include:

  • Processing commands.
  • Joining and leaving Discord voice channels.
  • Searching for requested music.
  • Maintaining a temporary music queue.
  • Controlling playback.
  • Preventing abuse and technical problems.
  • Maintaining and improving the service.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Theeram is designed to avoid retaining information longer than necessary for its functionality.

Temporary playback information, such as queues and voice connection state, may exist while a music session is active and may be cleared when the session ends.

If Theeram stores operational information for a longer period, that information will be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the service.

6. Sharing of Information

Theeram does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services when necessary to provide functionality, such as Discord and services used for music search or media retrieval.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies and terms. Theeram does not control how independent third-party services operate.

7. Discord API

Theeram uses Discord's API to communicate with Discord and provide its bot functionality.

Theeram's use of Discord data is subject to applicable Discord developer requirements and policies.

Discord itself may separately collect and process information according to its own privacy policy.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information handled by Theeram.

However, no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of personal information that Theeram may have stored and that can reasonably be associated with your use of the bot.

To request deletion, contact Theeram through our official Discord support server.

Request Support / Data Deletion →

Please do not send your Discord password, authentication token, or other sensitive credentials when making a request.

Some information may need to be retained where required by applicable law or where necessary for legitimate security or operational purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

Theeram does not intentionally request personal information from users for purposes unrelated to the bot's functionality.

Users must comply with Discord's applicable age requirements and applicable laws in their location.

11. Third-Party Links and Services

Theeram or this website may contain links to services operated by third parties.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party websites or services. You should review their respective privacy policies before using them.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Theeram's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

13. Discord Disclaimer

Theeram is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord Inc.

14. Contact & Data Requests

For privacy questions, support requests, or requests to modify or delete information associated with your use of Theeram, please contact us through our official Discord support server.
