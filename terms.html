2. About Theeram

Theeram is a Discord music bot designed to provide music-related functionality inside Discord servers.

Available functionality may include:

  • /play — Play a requested track.
  • /pause — Pause playback.
  • /resume — Resume playback.
  • /skip — Skip the current track.
  • /stop — Stop playback.
  • /queue — View the current queue.
  • /nowplaying — View the current track.
  • /volume — Adjust playback volume.
  • /disconnect — Disconnect from voice.
